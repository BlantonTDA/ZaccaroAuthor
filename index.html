Wordplay

A family card game by Lawrence M. Zaccaro. Make a new word or build on letters already played to score points.

How to play

Deal seven cards to each player and put the rest face down in a shared draw pile. The youngest player starts if they can make a word of at least three letters. After each turn, draw back up to seven cards while the pile lasts.

Keep score as you go. When the draw pile runs out, keep taking turns making any words you can. Subtract the value of any cards left in your hand. The highest score wins the round.

Follow the sample play

The first player makes OWL for 5 points. The next player lays down H and ING in front of themselves, with a gap for OWL, and announces HOWLING for 12 points. They score the full word.

The next two plays use ING to make BRING for 9 points and BR to make BREEZE for 12. The round continues with SNEEZE and BRICK, then a new word, ATE, for 3 points. The last player uses all seven of their cards with ATE to make OXYGENATED.
